Interview — Northline Counter app, trade customer reordering
Participant: P3, owner of a small building firm (fictional)
Interviewer: Northline designer

Interviewer: How do you usually order from Northline?

P3: Mostly at the counter, first thing in the morning. I know what I need, it's the same twenty items most weeks: screws, fixings, sealant, a couple of sizes of timber. I'd love to just press "same as last week".

Interviewer: Have you tried ordering in the Counter app?

P3: I tried. Search is fine if you know the exact name, but half my stuff I know by the bin number in the store, not the product name. So I end up scrolling, give up, and drive in.

Interviewer: What happens when you drive in?

P3: Queue, chat, they pull it, I sign the delivery note. It's twenty minutes I'm not on site. My lads text me photos of what they need and I forward them to the counter. That works, honestly, better than the app.

Interviewer: What would make the app worth using?

P3: Show me what I bought last time, let me change the numbers, done. And tell me if something's out of stock before I get there, not at the counter. Last month I drove forty minutes for sealant they didn't have.

Interviewer: Anything about paying?

P3: We're on account, so I don't care about cards. But I need the order to land on the right job number, otherwise my bookkeeper kills me.

Interviewer: Anything else?

P3: The app logs me out every few days. By the time I've found my password I've phoned the counter instead.
