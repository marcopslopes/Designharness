# Domain Taxonomy Example — Payments (fictional pack)

Use this as a reference for how to define product/domain taxonomy when
asking the agent to focus on a specific product area. **Confidence:
EXAMPLE — fictional codes/products, not real ones. Replace with your
own product's real taxonomy once you have one; do not treat these codes
as real feature names.**

When prompting the agent, include a taxonomy block like this if you want
signal tagging by product:

---

## Payments taxonomy (example)

- CP (Card Payment): card or phone wallet at the till. All markets.
- CR (Card Reader): Northline's own card terminal. Subset of markets.
- TTP (Tap to Pay): taking contactless payments on the store's phone. Subset of markets.
- PL (Pay Later): split or deferred payment for larger baskets. Subset of markets.
- TC (Trade Credit): invoiced payment terms for trade customers. All markets.
- GC (Gift Cards): store gift cards, sold and redeemed at the till. Subset of markets.
- RF (Refunds): full and partial refunds, in store or online. All markets.
- WL (Wallet): Northline Wallet — balance, payouts, transaction history. All markets.

---

For other domains (e.g. stock, supplier orders, onboarding), create a
similar list with short codes, descriptions, and market availability.
