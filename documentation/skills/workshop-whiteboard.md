# workshop-whiteboard

Facilitates design workshops - ideation, alignment, collaboration.

## What It Does

**Workshop facilitation** including:
- Agenda creation
- Activity design
- Miro/FigJam setup
- Facilitation guide
- Synthesis afterward

**Structured collaboration** - productive workshops.

---

## Workshop Types

### Ideation
Generate ideas, explore solutions.

### Alignment
Get team on same page.

### Prioritization
Decide what to build.

### Retrospective
Learn from past work.

---

## Example

```
You: "Create ideation workshop for referrals"

Output:
Agenda (90 min):
- 5 min: Context setting
- 10 min: How Might We statements
- 20 min: Crazy 8s ideation
- 15 min: Dot voting
- 20 min: Top 3 refinement
- 15 min: Playback + next steps
- 5 min: Retro

Miro board: Pre-populated with templates
Facilitation guide: What to say, when
Materials needed: Timer, virtual post-its
```
