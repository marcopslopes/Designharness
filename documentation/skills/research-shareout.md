# research-shareout

Presents research findings to stakeholders - clear, actionable, persuasive.

## What It Does

**Creates research presentations** that:
- Summarize key findings
- Tell a story
- Show evidence (quotes, data)
- Provide clear recommendations
- Drive decisions

**Stakeholder-ready** - makes research actionable.

---

## Shareout Structure

```
1. Context (2 min)
   - What we studied
   - Why
   - Methods

2. Key Findings (10 min)
   - Top 3-5 insights
   - Evidence for each
   - Quotes, screenshots

3. Recommendations (5 min)
   - Prioritized actions
   - Expected impact

4. Q&A (5 min)
   - Address concerns
   - Discuss trade-offs
```

---

## Example

```
You: "Create research shareout for city leaderboard"

Output: Deck with...
- Context: Validated motivation hypothesis
- Finding 1: 85% positive sentiment (quotes)
- Finding 2: Low-rank issue (data + quotes)
- Finding 3: Criteria confusion (screenshots)
- Recommendations: 3 actions, prioritized
- Next steps: Who, what, when
```
