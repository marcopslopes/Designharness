# insight-writer

Transforms observations into insights - the "so what?" layer.

## What It Does

**Turns data into insights** by:
- Identifying patterns
- Finding root causes
- Articulating implications
- Connecting to business goals

**From "what" to "so what."**

---

## Example

```
Observation: "68% of providers view leaderboard"
Insight: "Prominent placement drives engagement; competitive framing resonates with providers"

Observation: "Low-rank cities show 45% engagement"
Insight: "Leaderboard motivates winners but alienates losers; need progress indicators to maintain motivation across ranks"
```
