# research-summary

Quick research overview - TL;DR for stakeholders.

## What It Does

**One-page summary** with:
- Research question
- Methods used
- Key findings (3-5 bullets)
- Recommendations
- Next steps

**Fast consumption** - busy stakeholders can read in 2 minutes.

---

## Example

```
Research Summary: City Leaderboard Validation

Question: Is the leaderboard motivating?

Methods: Usability test (n=6), survey (n=200)

Key Findings:
✅ 68% engagement (exceeded goal)
✅ 85% positive sentiment
⚠️ Low-rank cities less engaged (45% vs 92%)

Recommendations:
1. Add progress indicators for low rankers
2. Make ranking criteria clearer

Next: Design updates (2 weeks), retest
```
