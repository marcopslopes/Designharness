# stakeholder-alignment

Maps stakeholders, identifies concerns, plans communication.

## What It Does

**Stakeholder management** by:
- Identifying key stakeholders
- Understanding their concerns
- Planning communication
- Addressing objections

**Political navigation** - design needs buy-in.

---

## Example

```
You: "Map stakeholders for city leaderboard"

Output:
Key Stakeholders:
1. Alex (PM)
   - Concern: Will it increase engagement?
   - Influence: High (approval required)
   - Strategy: Show research data

2. Jordan (Eng Lead)
   - Concern: API performance
   - Influence: High (implementation)
   - Strategy: Discuss caching strategy early

3. Sam (Support Lead)
   - Concern: Will users be confused?
   - Influence: Medium (feedback loop)
   - Strategy: Share usability test results

Communication Plan:
- Week 1: Present research to Alex + Jordan
- Week 2: Design review with full team
- Week 3: Support team preview
- Week 4: Final approval + kickoff
```
