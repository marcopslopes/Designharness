# Travel app

A first itinerary for someone who already knows where they're leaving from, where they're going, and how flexible the dates are. The plan is on the screen, and those three facts can be changed without leaving it.

## Itinerary

Lisbon to the Douro Valley, dates marked flexible. Day 4 is still open. Change edits the three facts. A stop opens its detail.
