# Everyday banking home

A concept home for a consumer banking app. It is for someone who opens the app to check how much they have and what just moved. The numbers are made up.

## Banking home

The balance sits first, then Add money, Send and Card, then the last five transactions grouped by day. Tap a row to see that payment. See all is on the screen and does not open a list yet.

![Banking home](screens/BankingHomePage.tsx)

## Send list

Send on the home opens this list. Search, add a recipient, then people you have already paid, with the account on the row. Tapping a person does not open an amount.

![Send list](screens/SendListPage.tsx)

## Login

A login for a customer this phone already knows. It asks for a password and shows the name, so you can see whose account it is. The name is made up. A correct password does not open the home yet.

![Login](screens/LoginPage.tsx)
