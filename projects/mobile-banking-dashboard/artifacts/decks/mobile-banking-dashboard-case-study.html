05
Problem · Why now

Providers can't tell
where their money is.

Owners and managers who take payments through Northline Pay can't tell from the wallet how much money is theirs, when it reaches their bank, or why some of it is held. So they call support. Some call several times about the same blocked wallet.

Why now. The wallet is already live, and support-call summaries from August to October 2026 kept describing the same confusion. A wallet entry point is also being built on the new profile screen.

The ask started as "a dashboard view for mobile banking".

The Slack sweep turned it into this narrower problem: the wallet exists, and it doesn't explain itself.

09
Evidence · Reference pattern

Monzo's pattern, challenged.

What Monzo does

One balance, three recent transactions

Monzo's personal app leads with one balance, previews three recent transactions, and shows money still to come next to money already spent.

Why not copy it

A provider's wallet isn't personal spending

It holds income, fees, tips, holds and payouts. The assumption map challenged copying the pattern directly.

The first build did follow this pattern. The next two slides show why it was dropped.

10
Options considered

Four questions, four calls.

What the screen is

ChosenThe Northline Wallet home, a provider's money screen

NotA generic banking home, the kickoff framing

What leads the screen

ChosenNext payout as the hero; Pending and On hold underneath

RejectedAn "Available" balance on top, with Next payout below

Where payout settings live

ChosenA visible "Payout Settings" row on the home

RejectedA gear icon in the header

Which platform

ChoseniOS only

RemovedAn Android screen inside the iPhone frame

Screen: the sweep found the wallet live and the pain already documented, so the wallet home won over a generic banking home.

Settings: the icon-only gear broke the style guide, and it hid a task providers already struggle with (signal 7).

Platform: the Android screen was the same body inside the iPhone frame, so the prototype stays on iOS.

The layout question gets its own slide next.

12
The decision

Lead with the next payout.

Direction built. A wallet home led by the next payout: its amount, date and progress from Scheduled to On the way to Arrived. Pending and On hold money sits underneath, and every hold is one tap from its reason and what happens next.

Why: it answers the brief's goal — how much, when, and why — in that order, and it removes the repeated figure.

Not validated. A design direction against an approved PRD. The wallet owner hasn't seen it.

One call: next payout first.

Be clear it's a direction, not a validated decision. Finance hasn't seen it.

14
When something's wrong · iOS
Payouts paused
Failed payout
Not set up

Paused: payouts stopped until the provider confirms their identity.

Failed: the bank returned a payout; the money goes into the next one.

Not set up: no payout account yet, with three steps to set one up.
